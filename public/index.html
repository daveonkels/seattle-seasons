Seattle Seasons

What is this?

An interactive field guide to Seattle's 14 unofficial seasons. It started as a deadpan civic-style poster for new arrivals; this version lets the weather move as you scrub through the year.

Drag, scroll, swipe, or use the left and right arrow keys to move season by season.
